Sharing the INDMoney Weekly Review Pulse, based on public App Store and Google Play reviews from 27 June – 18 September 2026. We analyzed 1,323 reviews in total. The top three themes, user quotes, and recommended actions are summarized below.

TOP 3 THEMES

• App Performance & UX — 272 reviews · 20.6%
• Investing, Trading & Portfolio — 207 reviews · 15.6%
• Money Movement & Payments — 105 reviews · 7.9%

REAL USER VOICE

• “dashboards are not clear,”

• “Stock trade executed notification ( push notifications) not working at all. After many complaints and calls also no improvement. i get all other useless push notifications, but not trade executed notification, which is THE most important for a trader. US stock deposite process fails most of the time. also interface of portfolio and trading needs to improve a lot. kindly look in notification issue”

• “Cannot use any bank to transfer other than 5 listed by them. There's no way I'm gonna create new bank accounts just to trade.”

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

1. Improve dashboard clarity. Audit high-friction dashboard flows and validate simpler information hierarchy and navigation with users.

2. Improve trade-notification reliability. Audit the trade-execution notification flow, identify delivery failures, and track successful notification delivery.

3. Reduce transfer friction. Analyze unsupported-bank demand and improve the transfer experience for users affected by bank-coverage limitations.

Methodology: Public review exports from Google Play and the App Store were combined for the 12-week period. Reviews were grouped into a maximum of five themes. Generic or insufficiently detailed reviews were not forced into a theme. No usernames, emails, IDs, or other PII are included in this pulse.
Source dataset: INDMoney review export used for Challenge 5 analysis.
